Clinical Indications

Signs You Need a Tooth Filling

Catching tooth decay early prevents painful nerve infection and avoids the need for Root Canal Treatment (RCT) or extractions.

Hot & Cold Sensitivity

A sharp jolt of pain when drinking cold water, eating hot momos, or having sugary sweets.

Visible Dark Spots / Holes

Brown, black, or grey discoloration, pits, or hollow spots on the chewing surfaces of your teeth.

Food Getting Trapped

Food particles repeatedly getting wedged between the same teeth due to hidden interproximal decay.

Chipped / Fractured Edge

Rough or sharp tooth edges caused by biting hard food, sports trauma, or enamel erosion.

Why Nano-Hybrid Composite is Superior

Material Comparison

Composite vs Silver Amalgam vs GIC

Understand the differences between modern dental restorative materials.

Tooth-Colored Composite

Recommended Choice

Directly bonds to enamel and dentin, requiring minimal tooth shaving. Completely invisible, 100% mercury-free, and instantly hardened under LED blue light.

  • ✓ Exact natural shade match
  • ✓ Strengthens natural tooth structure
  • ✓ Eat immediately after appointment
  • ✓ Ideal for both front and back teeth

Old Silver Amalgam

Outdated Technology

Contains ~50% elemental mercury combined with silver and tin. Requires cutting healthy tooth structure for mechanical retention, darkens teeth, and causes hairline fractures.

  • Unattractive black/metallic appearance
  • Contains mercury alloy
  • Thermal expansion risks tooth fracture
  • Requires 24 hours to set

Glass Ionomer (GIC)

Fluoride Releasing

Forms a chemical bond with tooth structure while continuously releasing fluoride ions to protect against recurring caries. Best suited for baby teeth, cervical root decay, and temporary dressings.

  • ✓ Continuous fluoride protection
  • ✓ Excellent for pediatric milk teeth
  • ✓ Great for root surface decay
  • ✓ Affordable rates (NPR 800 – 2,500)

Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step Gentle Filling Process

A fast, comfortable, and meticulous restorative protocol designed for lasting durability.

01

Digital RVG Diagnosis

High-resolution low-radiation digital X-ray pinpointing the exact depth of decay and proximity to the dental pulp nerve.

02

Painless Numbing & Prep

Topical gel followed by gentle local anesthesia ensures you feel zero discomfort while decay and infected dentin are removed.

03

Micro-Etching & Bonding

Microscopic pores are created on the enamel, followed by an adhesive bonding agent that chemically locks the filling to the tooth.

04

Layering & LED Blue Curing

Shade-matched nano-composite is placed in fine anatomical layers, with each layer instantly hardened in seconds by blue LED light.

05

Occlusal Bite Check & Polish

We check your bite with articulating paper and buff the restoration with diamond polishing points for a smooth, natural feel.
