Why Choose Zirconia Over Metal-Ceramic Crowns?

Traditional porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) crowns often show dark grey lines near the gums over time. Monolithic Zirconia is 100% tooth-colored, ultra-strong (1200+ MPa strength), and never shows metal margins.
