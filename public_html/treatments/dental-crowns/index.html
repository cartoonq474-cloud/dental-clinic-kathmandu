Clinical Indications

When Does a Tooth Need a Dental Crown?

Dental crowns provide a 360-degree protective helmet that preserves damaged natural teeth from extraction.

After Root Canal (RCT)

Dead teeth become brittle over time. A crown prevents the tooth from cracking down into the jawbone.

Heavily Broken Teeth

Restores chewing function and seals teeth that have lost large cusps from trauma or chewing hard foods.

Replacing Failing Fillings

When a cavity consumes more than 60% of tooth structure, a filling is too weak and a crown is necessary.

Dental Implant Caps

Precision screw-retained or cement-retained Zirconia crowns completing a dental implant restoration.

Why Choose Zirconia Over Metal PFM Crowns?

Traditional porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM) crowns rely on a dark grey alloy base that inevitably shows a dark metal shadow along your gumline as gums naturally age. Furthermore, the porcelain shell on PFM crowns frequently chips off under heavy biting.

Monolithic Zirconia is a single solid block of ceramic diamond-hard crystal (1200+ MPa). It is 100% metal-free, completely immune to chipping, and never exposes dark metal margins.

Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step CAD/CAM Crown Workflow

A precision 2-visit restorative protocol designed for flawless fit, comfort, and zero sensitivity.

01

Micro-Tooth Preparation

Under local anesthesia, we gently shape the tooth margins, removing only the minimal enamel required for crown placement.

02

3D Optical Intraoral Scan

No messy goopy impression trays. Our high-speed intraoral scanner captures a 3D digital model of your prepared tooth in seconds.

03

Temporary Crown Protection

We place a comfortable temporary tooth crown to protect your tooth and maintain chewing while your permanent crown is milled.

04

CAD/CAM 5-Axis Robotic Milling

Your crown is precision-milled from a German Zirconia block and sintered in a 1500°C furnace for extreme structural density.

05

Permanent Adhesive Cementation

We verify the contact points and shade match, then permanently bond the crown with high-strength resin cement.
