What Are Dental Veneers?

Dental veneers are ultra-thin shells crafted from porcelain or composite resins that are custom-bonded to the front surface of your teeth. Veneers instantly correct esthetic imperfections, including severe discoloration, gaps, alignment issues, or chipped teeth, delivering a balanced, natural-looking smile makeover.

Who Needs Dental Veneers?

Veneers are a premium choice for resolving multiple visual dental defects. You should consider veneers if you have:

  • Chipped, worn, or cracked front teeth.
  • Gaps (diastema) between your front teeth.
  • Teeth that are slightly crooked or misshapen.
  • Severe stains that do not respond to chemical whitening.

Benefits of Dental Veneers

  • ✓ Instant correction: Corrects color, alignment, and gaps simultaneously.
  • ✓ Natural look: Porcelain mimics the light-reflecting properties of real enamel.
  • ✓ Stain resistance: Porcelain shells resist discoloration from coffee and tea.
  • ✓ Durable restoration: With proper care, veneers can last 10 to 15 years.

Step-by-Step Veneer Procedure

  • 1

    Consultation & Smile Design

    We take digital photographs, impressions, and design a custom smile blueprint matching your facial features.

  • 2

    Tooth Preparation

    A minimal layer of enamel (about 0.5mm) is prepared to accommodate the veneer thickness, and a final impression is sent to the lab.

  • 3

    Temporary Veneers

    Temporary acrylic veneers are fitted to protect your prepared teeth while the lab crafts your custom porcelain shells.

  • 4

    Final Bonding

    The dentist evaluates the shape, shade, and fit of the custom porcelain veneers, then permanently bonds them to your teeth.

Recovery & Aftercare

Veneers require zero down-time. You can chew and speak normally as soon as the local anesthetic wears off. Avoid biting on hard objects (e.g., ice, fingernails) with your front teeth and maintain standard brushing and flossing habits daily.

Starting Cost

Our composite veneers start at **NPR 4,500** per tooth, and laboratory porcelain veneers start at **NPR 15,000** per tooth.
