Our 4-Step Clinical Whitening Process

What to expect during your 60-minute visit at our Putalisadak dental clinic.

01

Checkup & Polish

Frequently Asked Questions in Kathmandu

Everything you need to know about teeth whitening at BrightSmile Dental Clinic.

Our complete in-office blue LED teeth whitening session at BrightSmile Dental Clinic in Putalisadak costs NPR 10,000. This all-inclusive package covers pre-whitening oral assessment, gum barrier protection, LED light session, and desensitizing fluoride coating.

We are centrally located at Putalisadak (opposite Kumari Bank), Kathmandu. We are easily reachable within 5–10 minutes from Bagbazar, New Road, Durbar Marg, and Kamaladi. Dedicated two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking spaces are available for our patients.

Yes! Professional in-office whitening is completely safe when performed under dental supervision. We use pH-balanced bleaching gels and protective liquid dams to shield your gums and enamel throughout the session.

Results typically last between 1 to 2 years. To maintain your bright smile longer, we recommend brushing twice daily, reducing consumption of heavy staining drinks (like strong black tea or red wine), and coming in for regular 6-month cleanings.

Some patients experience minor, temporary sensitivity for 12–24 hours post-treatment. We apply a specialized fluoride desensitizing gel immediately after your whitening session to minimize any sensation.
