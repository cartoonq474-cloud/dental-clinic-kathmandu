The Science of Gentle Micro-Forces

Clear aligners work by applying calibrated, gentle continuous pressure to specific teeth in micro-stages (0.25mm per tray). Each set of custom BPA-free trays is worn for 10 to 14 days before progressing to the next stage.

Because they are 100% removable, you can eat your favorite Nepali foods, attend wedding celebrations without braces, and maintain flawless oral hygiene without wire threaders.
