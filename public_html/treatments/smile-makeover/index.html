Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step Digital Smile Design Process

Precision cosmetic methodology ensuring complete predictability and zero surprises.

01

Facial Photography & 3D Scan

We capture studio portrait photography, video dynamics, and high-speed optical 3D intraoral scans of your dental arches.

02

3D Digital Smile Architecture

Using Digital Smile Design software, we architect your new tooth shapes, widths, and contours to match your facial aesthetics.

03

Live "Trial Smile" Mock-Up

We place a temporary aesthetic mock-up directly over your natural teeth so you can see and feel your new smile in the mirror.

04

Minimal Prep & Artisan Lab Milling

We gently buff 0.3mm of enamel and our master dental ceramists craft your restorations using genuine German E.max porcelain.

05

Permanent Bonding & Polish

Restorations are permanently fused with aesthetic dual-cure resin cement, balanced for perfect chewing, and polished to a diamond shine.
