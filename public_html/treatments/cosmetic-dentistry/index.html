What Is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry includes specialized dental procedures designed to improve the look, color, position, and overall aesthetics of your teeth. At our Kathmandu clinic, we focus on delivering natural-looking, beautiful smile designs that align with your facial features and bite structure, using conservative and safe treatment methods.

Who Needs Cosmetic Dental Treatment?

Esthetic dental treatments are ideal for individuals looking to resolve issues such as:

  • Severely discolored, yellowed, or stained teeth.
  • Chipped, worn, or fractured teeth.
  • Gaps, minor crowding, or misaligned spacing.
  • Misshapen teeth or uneven gumlines.

Benefits of Cosmetic Smile Design

  • ✓ Enhanced Self-Confidence: A bright, balanced smile increases personal and professional confidence.
  • ✓ Improved Dental Function: Repairing chipped or worn teeth restores correct biting alignment.
  • ✓ Conservative Approach: Veneers and whitening preserve the maximum amount of natural enamel.
  • ✓ Tailored Design: Custom-designed shells and crowns matched to your color preference.

Our Cosmetic Services in Kathmandu

We provide a comprehensive range of esthetic options to help you achieve your smile goals:

  • Professional Teeth Whitening: Light-activated bleaching sessions that brighten your smile by up to 8 shades in a single clinical visit.
  • Custom Dental Veneers: Thin porcelain or composite shells bonded to the front of teeth to instantly cover chips, gaps, and stains.
  • Smile Makeovers: A complete, coordinated treatment combination (whitening, veneers, and braces) to completely redesign your smile.
  • Aesthetic Ceramic Crowns: Metal-free porcelain and zirconia crowns to restore heavily damaged front teeth with absolute natural translucency.
