General Treatment Questions

We recommend a routine dental examination and professional teeth cleaning every six months to identify early tooth decay and prevent plaque accumulation.

Most cosmetic procedures, such as teeth whitening and veneers, are non-invasive or require only minor local anesthesia. We ensure your comfort throughout the session.

A dental bridge treatment typically requires two appointments: one for diagnostics and tooth preparation, and another for cementing the custom permanent bridge.
