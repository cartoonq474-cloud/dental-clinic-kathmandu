Why Waiting for Toothache Costs 10x More

Tooth decay and gum disease are silent invaders. Enamel has no nerve endings, so a cavity can eat deep into your tooth for months without causing a single drop of pain—until it strikes the sensitive nerve.

Catching a micro-cavity during a NPR 1,000 checkup requires a simple 20-minute filling (NPR 1,500), whereas waiting until severe pain develops leads to a Root Canal Treatment and Crown (NPR 20,000+).
