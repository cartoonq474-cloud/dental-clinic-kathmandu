What Is Teeth Cleaning and Scaling?

Professional teeth cleaning (scaling and polishing) is a clinical hygiene procedure designed to remove plaque and hardened tartar (calculus) that standard brushing cannot clear. Tartar deposits harbor bacteria that cause gum inflammation (gingivitis), bad breath, and eventual bone loss around the teeth. We complete the treatment with clinical polishing for a smooth finish.

Who Needs This Treatment?

Calculus accumulates gradually over time. You should schedule a professional clean if you notice:

  • Yellow or brown hard deposits along your gumline.
  • Gums that bleed when flossing or brushing.
  • Puffy, tender, or swollen gums.
  • Persistent bad taste or odor in your mouth.

Benefits of Scaling

  • ✓ Complete removal of tartar and bacteria, reducing gum inflammation.
  • ✓ Eliminates chronic bad breath caused by food residue.
  • ✓ Polishes away tea, coffee, and tobacco stains for a clean smile.
  • ✓ Vital in preventing gum recession and teeth loosening.

Step-by-Step Cleaning Procedure

  • 1

    Ultrasonic Scaling

    We use an ultrasonic scaler that emits mild vibrations and a cooling water mist to break down and wash away hardened tartar deposits.

  • 2

    Manual Fine Scaling

    The dentist uses handheld scaling instruments to carefully clean hard-to-reach pockets between the teeth and under the gumline.

  • 3

    Prophy-Polishing

    A soft polishing cup with fluoridated clinical paste is used to buff the teeth, removing surface stains and smoothing the enamel.

Recovery & Aftercare

Your teeth and gums may feel slightly sensitive to hot or cold temperatures for 24-48 hours. We recommend:

  • Avoiding extremely hot, cold, or spicy foods for the first day.
  • Brushing gently with a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Flossing daily to prevent plaque from forming on clean surfaces.
