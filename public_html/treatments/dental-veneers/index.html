Material Comparison

E.max Porcelain vs Composite Veneers vs Crowns

Understand the aesthetic and structural differences between cosmetic options.

E.max® Porcelain Veneers

Gold Standard

Ultra-thin glass-ceramic custom milled in lab. 100% stain-proof, lifelike light refraction, and 500+ MPa compressive strength.

  • ✓ 15 to 20+ year lifespan
  • ✓ Impervious to tea, coffee & curry
  • ✓ Minimal prep (0.3–0.5mm enamel only)
  • ✓ 5-Year clinical warranty included

Direct Composite Veneers

Single-Visit Budget

Sculpted directly onto teeth by dentist in 1 visit using nano-hybrid resin. Reversible, budget-friendly, but requires occasional polishing.

  • ✓ Fast same-day completion
  • ✓ Budget friendly (NPR 3,500 – 6,500)
  • ✓ Easily repaired if chipped
  • ✓ 5 to 7 year lifespan

Zirconia / Porcelain Crowns

360° Structural Cap

Encompasses the entire tooth structure. Ideal for heavily broken teeth, severe decay, or teeth that have had root canal therapy.

  • ✓ Full 360° chewing reinforcement
  • ✓ 1200+ MPa fracture resistance
  • ✓ Requires 1.0–1.5mm tooth trimming
  • ✓ Rates: NPR 15,000 – 28,000
Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step Digital Smile Design Process

A precision cosmetic protocol designed for natural beauty, perfect fit, and zero pain.

01

Digital Smile Simulation

We take studio portrait photos, analyze facial proportions, and create a 3D digital simulation of your ideal smile preview.

02

Ultra-Conservative Micro-Prep

We gently buff 0.3mm to 0.5mm of outer enamel under magnification and place comfortable temporary trial veneers.

03

3D Digital Intraoral Scan

No messy goopy impression trays. Our high-speed optical 3D scanner captures every microscopic margin digitally.

04

CAD/CAM Robotic Milling

Genuine German E-Max lithium disilicate blocks are robotic milled and hand-glazed with translucent ceramic gradients.

05

Permanent Adhesive Fusion

Veneers are bonded with light-cured aesthetic resin cement, checked for perfect bite alignment, and polished to a diamond shine.

Dental Veneers FAQs

Veneers are ultra-thin (0.3–0.5mm) custom shells of German IPS E.max® glass ceramic bonded to the front of teeth to correct deep stains, chips, gaps, and worn edges with minimal tooth preparation.

Composite veneers are hand-sculpted in a single visit at a lower cost (NPR 4,000–7,000 per tooth). Porcelain E.max veneers are lab-crafted, 100% stain-proof, and last 15–20 years (NPR 12,000–25,000 per tooth).
