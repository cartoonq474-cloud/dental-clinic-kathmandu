Our Story

Dental in Kathmandu was founded in Putalisadak, Kathmandu with a single vision: to deliver premium, reliable, and accessible dental care to the local community. Over the last 10 years, we have grown from a small clinic into a fully equipped modern dental care center, serving more than 5,000 satisfied patients.

Our team of highly qualified dental specialists collaborates to offer integrated diagnostic and surgical dental solutions under strict infection-control standards.

Our Mission

To restore health and confidence through advanced treatments, ethical diagnostics, and compassionate patient care.

Our Vision

To set the benchmark for modern dental care in Nepal by introducing innovative dental technology and continuous training.
