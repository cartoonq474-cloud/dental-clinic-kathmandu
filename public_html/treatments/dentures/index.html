Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step Custom Denture Workflow

A precise, senior-friendly clinical process ensuring airtight suction, bite balance, and natural aesthetics.

01

Primary Anatomical Impression

We take gentle impressions of your gum ridges to evaluate bone architecture and construct custom individual impression trays.

02

Border Molding & Secondary Scan

Using thermo-plastic border molding material, we capture dynamic lip and cheek movements for airtight suction seal retention.

03

Jaw Bite & Smile Registration

We measure your exact vertical jaw height, lip support, and select tooth shades matching your age and facial skin tone.

04

Aesthetic Wax Try-In Preview

You test the teeth set in wax in the mirror to verify tooth size, smile symmetry, and speech phonetics before permanent curing.

05

Final Delivery & Free Adjustments

We deliver your high-gloss cured dentures and schedule complimentary follow-up visits to relieve any initial pressure spots.

Dentures FAQs

Guidance on complete, partial, and flexible dentures in Kathmandu, Nepal.

Complete dentures replace all missing teeth in a fully toothless upper or lower arch. Partial dentures replace several missing teeth while securely clasping onto your remaining natural teeth.

BPS (Biofunctional Prosthetic System) dentures provide unmatched suction and bite stability. Flexible Valplast dentures are lightweight, unbreakable, and have gum-colored clasps with zero visible metal.

Standard acrylic complete dentures cost NPR 15,000–25,000 per arch. Premium BPS dentures cost NPR 35,000–55,000. Flexible partial dentures start from NPR 12,000–22,000.

Most patients adapt within 2 to 3 weeks. Start with soft foods cut into small bites, chew on both sides simultaneously, and practice reading aloud to quickly regain speech clarity.

No. Always remove your dentures before sleeping to allow your gums and jaw tissues to breathe. Store them in a container of clean water or denture-cleaning solution overnight.

Without tooth roots, the jawbone gradually shrinks (bone resorption) over time. Loose dentures can be relined or rebased at our clinic to restore a snug, comfortable suction fit.
