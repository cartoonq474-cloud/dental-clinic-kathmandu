Diastema Causes

Why Do Gaps Form Between Front Teeth?

Understanding the anatomical cause allows us to recommend the ideal, permanent gap closure method.

Thick Labial Frenum

A thick muscular tissue band connecting the upper lip to the gums that prevents front teeth from touching.

Peg Lateral Incisors

Undersized lateral incisors that leave noticeable empty spacing on either side of the front teeth.

Jaw-Tooth Discrepancy

A broad dental arch paired with small natural teeth, creating generalized spacing throughout the mouth.

Tongue Thrusting

Habitual pressure against front teeth when swallowing or talking that pushes teeth outwards over time.

Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step Same-Day Gap Closure Procedure

A relaxed, comfortable 45-minute cosmetic appointment with instant transformation.

01

Digital Shade Matching

We match the exact hue, opacity, and light value to your natural teeth under color-corrected clinical lighting.

02

Micro-Etch Conditioning

We apply a gentle phosphoric etching gel to prepare enamel pores for bonding. Zero healthy tooth enamel is drilled away.

03

Artistic Resin Sculpting

Our cosmetic dentist layers nano-hybrid resin incrementally, creating anatomically perfect contact points and tooth curves.

04

LED Light Polymerization

A high-intensity blue LED light hardens the composite material in seconds, creating a permanent adhesive bond.

05

Diamond Mirror Polish

We polish the restoration using micro-fine diamond discs and polishing paste to achieve a glass-smooth, stain-resistant shine.
