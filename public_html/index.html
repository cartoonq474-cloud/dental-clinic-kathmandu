About Our Clinic

Your Trusted Partner for Exceptional Dental Care in Kathmandu

Established in 2008 in Putalisadak, BrightSmile Dental Clinic has been dedicated to providing premium quality dental care for patients of all ages. Our commitment to comfort, advanced techniques, and personalized treatment plans ensures a dental experience that will make you smile.

We house specialized dentists focused on dental implants, teeth whitening, orthodontics, root canals, and pediatric care using certified sterilization procedures.
