Facts vs Myths

Common Myths About Teeth Scaling

Separating medical facts from traditional misconceptions about dental cleaning.

Myth #1

"Scaling removes tooth enamel and thins teeth."

Medical Fact

Ultrasonic tips vibrate against calculus crystals without scratching or cutting healthy enamel. Tooth enamel is the hardest substance in the human body and cannot be damaged by calibrated scaling.

Myth #2

"Scaling creates permanent gaps between teeth."

Medical Fact

Tartar deposits act like cement, filling the natural interdental gaps and pushing gums back. When the infection is removed, you simply feel your natural spaces. Swollen gums heal and tighten within days.

Myth #3

"Scaling makes teeth loose and shaky."

Medical Fact

Neglected tartar destroys supporting jawbone, causing mobile teeth. Leaving tartar on will guarantee tooth loss. Scaling stops active bone loss and allows gums to reattach firmly.
