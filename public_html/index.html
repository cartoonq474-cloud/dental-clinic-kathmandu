About Our Clinic

Your Trusted Partner for Dental Care in Kathmandu

At Dental in Kathmandu, we have been delivering premier, modern dental care in Kathmandu since 2016. From our fully-equipped clinic in Putalisadak, our mission is to provide comprehensive, ethical, and patient-first dentistry. We combine years of clinical experience with advanced diagnostic tools to ensure long-term oral health for every patient.

Our team of highly qualified dental specialists holds MDS degrees and professional NMC registrations, collaborating to offer integrated services—ranging from routine checkups to smile transformations. Over the last decade, we have proudly treated more than 5,000 satisfied patients. By adapting state-of-the-art technologies such as digital low-radiation X-rays, intraoral scanners, and modern autoclave sterilization systems, we ensure a safe, comfortable, and painless dental experience.
