Clinical Indications

When Is Tooth Extraction Necessary?

We prioritize saving natural teeth with fillings and root canals. Extraction is recommended only when conservative treatments are no longer viable.

Severe Subgingival Decay

Cavities extending deeply below the bone level where a crown or filling can no longer seal.

Vertical Root Fracture

Cracks that split the root down the center, which cannot be sealed by root canal therapy.

🩸

Advanced Mobility

Severe periodontal bone loss causing teeth to become painfully loose and unable to support chewing.

Orthodontic Crowding

Planned extraction of select premolars to create room for aligning severe dental crowding.

Why Atraumatic Bone Preservation Matters

Traditional rough extractions expand and fracture the delicate outer jawbone socket walls, leaving thin, sunken ridges that make future dental implants difficult or impossible.

Our atraumatic extraction protocol uses precision micro-luxators to sever the microscopic periodontal ligament fibers gently. The tooth slips out smoothly with zero bone trauma, keeping your jawbone intact for future implants or bridges.

Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step Gentle Extraction Process

A quick, sterile procedure ensuring maximum comfort and rapid socket healing.

01

Digital RVG Radiographic Diagnosis

We analyze root curvature, number of roots, and surrounding bone density on digital X-rays to plan a smooth, gentle extraction.

02

Effective Local Anesthesia Numbing

Targeted anesthesia is administered gently. We confirm 100% total numbness before beginning so you feel zero sharp sensations.

03

Micro-Luxator Periodontal Release

Using delicate micro-luxators, we separate the periodontal ligament fibers cleanly, loosening the tooth with minimal force.

04

Atraumatic Tooth Elevation

The tooth is gently lifted out intact without breaking the surrounding alveolar bone plate or tearing adjacent gums.

05

Socket Debridement & Gauze Pack

The socket is cleaned, disinfected, and packed with sterile gauze. We provide written post-operative healing instructions.
