What Is a Dental Checkup?

A dental checkup is a comprehensive diagnostic scan of your teeth, gums, and mouth structures. At Dental in Kathmandu, this includes cavity detection, digital X-rays to scan below the enamel, periodontal pocket measurement, oral cancer screens, and custom dental guidance from our specialist dentists.

Who Needs This Treatment?

Everyone should undergo a routine clinical dental examination every six months. You specifically need an immediate checkup if you experience:

  • Sensitivity to hot or cold drinks.
  • Bleeding or tender gums while brushing.
  • Chronic bad breath (halitosis) or localized tooth pain.
  • Dark spots or visual cavities on the chewing surfaces.

Benefits of Regular Checkups

  • ✓ Early cavity and tooth decay detection before they cause pain.
  • ✓ Preventive diagnostics saving you from complex procedures.
  • ✓ Professional examination of existing fillings and crowns.
  • ✓ Complete oral health check keeping your gums firm and clean.

Step-by-Step Checkup Procedure

  • 1

    Visual Examination

    The dentist scans all teeth surfaces with high-precision dental mirrors to identify visible decay, plaque, and tooth wear patterns.

  • 2

    Periodontal Gums Scan

    We check for symptoms of gum disease (gingivitis) by examining gum firmness and measuring periodontal pockets.

  • 3

    Digital X-Ray (If Needed)

    Digital imaging is taken to check for bone decay, impacted wisdom teeth, or cavities hidden between the teeth.

  • 4

    Consultation & Treatment Plan

    A complete oral summary is prepared, and the dentist discusses home care habits or scheduled treatments if needed.

Recovery & Aftercare

A dental checkup is a non-invasive diagnostic procedure. There is zero recovery time, and you can resume standard eating, drinking, and regular activities immediately after leaving the clinic chair.
