Treatment Steps

Our 5-Stage Orthodontic Workflow

A structured, personalized plan to guide your teeth into optimal cosmetic and functional alignment.

01

Diagnostic Ceph & OPG X-Rays

We take digital panoramic & lateral cephalometric X-rays and study casts to calculate exact tooth angulations and jaw metrics.

02

Painless Bracket Bonding

Our MDS Orthodontist bonds brackets to each tooth using dental adhesive and inserts the initial flexible NiTi archwire.

03

Monthly Aligning Adjustments

Quick 20-minute clinic visits every 4–6 weeks for wire changes, power chains, and tracking tooth repositioning.

04

Finishing & Bite Detailing

Final microscopic adjustments to ensure perfect contact between upper and lower molars before bracket removal.

05

Debonding & Clear Retainers

We remove brackets, polish your enamel to a high shine, and fit custom retainers to lock your straight smile permanently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Orthodontic Braces FAQs

Guide to metal, ceramic, and self-ligating braces in Kathmandu.

Metal braces use high-grade stainless steel brackets for maximum durability. Ceramic braces use tooth-colored translucent ceramic brackets that blend in naturally with your teeth.

Routine archwire adjustments and ligature changes take place once every 4 to 6 weeks at our Putalisadak orthodontic center.

Traditional Metal Braces cost NPR 40,000–60,000. Aesthetic Ceramic Braces cost NPR 55,000–85,000. Self-ligating Damon-style braces cost NPR 75,000–1,10,000 with flexible monthly installments.

Avoid hard and sticky foods like hard candies, whole apples (slice them first), chewing gum, hard crusts, and nuts that can pop brackets off teeth.

Treatment takes 12 to 24 months depending on crowding and bite complexity. Following your orthodontist’s instructions ensures the fastest, most stable alignment.
