What Is a Dental Crown?

A dental crown (commonly called a tooth cap) is a custom-fitted restoration that covers the entire visible portion of a damaged, weak, or decayed tooth. Crowns restore a tooth's natural shape, size, strength, and appearance. At BrightSmile, we offer premium metal-ceramic crowns, full ceramic crowns, and metal-free Zirconia crowns.

Who Needs a Dental Crown?

Crowns are restorative and protective. You typically need a crown if you have:

  • A tooth weakened by a large cavity or fracture.
  • A tooth that has undergone root canal treatment.
  • A severely discolored or misshapen tooth.
  • An implant structure that needs a final tooth cap.

Benefits of Dental Crowns

  • ✓ Complete protection: Prevents weak or fractured teeth from breaking further.
  • ✓ Natural looks: Shaded and shaped to align with your surrounding teeth.
  • ✓ Highly durable: Resists strong biting forces, lasting up to 10-15 years.
  • ✓ Restores bite: Allows you to chew and speak comfortably without pain.

Step-by-Step Crown Procedure

  • 1

    Preparation & Shaping

    The dentist prepares and shapes the outer layer of the tooth to accommodate the crown thickness. Local anesthesia is used to ensure comfort.

  • 2

    Impression Take

    We take high-resolution dental impressions of the prepared tooth and surrounding bite, which are sent to our laboratory.

  • 3

    Temporary Crown Fit

    A temporary crown is bonded to protect your shaped tooth while the lab crafts your custom permanent crown.

  • 4

    Permanent Cementation

    During your second visit, the temporary cap is removed, the fit and color of the permanent crown are verified, and it is cemented in place.

Recovery & Aftercare

Avoid chewing extremely sticky or hard foods (e.g., gum, hard candy) while wearing your temporary crown. Once the permanent crown is cemented, maintain normal brushing and flossing. Pay extra attention to the gumline around the crown to prevent plaque buildup.

Frequently Asked Questions

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, dental crowns typically last between 10 to 15 years.

Root canal treatment removes the inner blood supply of a tooth, making it dry and brittle over time. A crown covers and reinforces the tooth to prevent it from cracking under biting forces.

PFM crowns have a metal core covered by porcelain; they are strong but can show a dark metal line at the gumline over time. Zirconia crowns are 100% metal-free, extremely strong, highly aesthetic, and look identical to natural teeth.

Some minor sensitivity to temperature is normal for a few days after cementation. If the sensitivity persists, consult your dentist.

While rare, dental cement can wash out or a crown can loosen. If your crown falls off, keep it clean and contact us immediately to have it re-cemented.
