What Are Dental Fillings?

Dental fillings are restorative treatments used to repair teeth damaged by decay, chipping, or wear. At Dental in Kathmandu, we use premium tooth-colored composite resins. The composite material bonds directly to your natural tooth structures, preserving strength and matching the color of your teeth.

Who Needs Dental Fillings?

You may need a filling if decay has created a hole (cavity) in your tooth. Key symptoms include:

  • Sharp pain when biting or chewing food.
  • Sensitivity when consuming sweet, hot, or cold foods.
  • A visible dark hole or crack in a tooth.
  • Food constantly getting trapped in specific spots.

Benefits of Composite Fillings

  • ✓ Natural appearance: Shaded to match your surrounding tooth color.
  • ✓ Chemical bonding: Adheres directly, providing reinforcement.
  • ✓ Conservative prep: Requires removing less healthy tooth structure than silver fillings.
  • ✓ Mercury-free: 100% safe, biocompatible composite resin materials.

Step-by-Step Procedure

  • 1

    Numbing & Isolation

    We apply a local anesthetic to ensure a painless experience, then isolate the area to keep the tooth clean and dry during treatment.

  • 2

    Decay Removal

    The dentist removes the decayed portion of the tooth, leaving only healthy, clean tooth structure.

  • 3

    Filling Placement

    The composite resin is applied in layers, with each layer cured using a specialized blue light to harden it instantly.

  • 4

    Shaping & Polishing

    The dentist shapes the filling to match your bite, then polishes it to a smooth finish that feels natural to your tongue.

Recovery & Aftercare

Because composite fillings are cured instantly, you can chew on them immediately. However, if a local anesthetic was used, we recommend waiting until the numbness wears off to avoid biting your cheek or tongue. Some temporary sensitivity to cold foods is normal for a few days.
