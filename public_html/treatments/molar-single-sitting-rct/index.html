Treatment Selection

Single-Sitting vs Multi-Visit RCT: Which is Right for You?

We tailor the protocol to your tooth’s clinical biology to guarantee the highest long-term success rate.

Single-Sitting RCT

1 Visit (60–90 min)

Everything completed in a single appointment under sterile rubber dam isolation. Saves time, eliminates multiple injections, and gets you back to work immediately.

Ideal Candidates:

  • ✓ Acute irreversible pulpitis (severe sharp pain)
  • ✓ Fractured or chipped tooth exposing live nerve
  • ✓ Intentional RCT for prosthetic crown/bridge
  • ✓ Clean dry canals with no active pus discharge

Multi-Visit RCT

2 Visits (40 min each)

Medication (Calcium Hydroxide) is placed inside the canals between visits to neutralize severe bacterial infections before final sealing.

Recommended For:

  • ✓ Large periapical abscess with visible bone loss
  • ✓ Active continuous weeping/pus drainage
  • ✓ Severe facial swelling requiring antibiotics
  • ✓ Complex re-treatment of previously failed root canals

Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step Single-Sitting Molar RCT Protocol

A meticulous, pain-free procedure performed by Senior Endodontists.

01

Digital RVG & Canal Mapping

We analyze 3D root curvature and locate all primary and accessory canals (including MB2) using digital radiovisiography.

02

Painless Anesthesia & Dam Isolation

Long-acting local nerve block numbing ensures 100% comfort. A sterile rubber dam isolates the tooth from oral bacteria.

03

Motorized Rotary NiTi Shaping

Swiss-manufactured rotary files gently clean, widen, and shape curved molar canals without straining root walls.

04

Ultrasonic 3D Sterilization

Sonic activation drives warm antibacterial irrigants deep into lateral canals, eliminating 99.9% of microbial biofilm.

05

Thermoplastic 3D Obturation

Canals are hermetically filled with biocompatible warm gutta-percha, followed by a composite core and crown prep.
