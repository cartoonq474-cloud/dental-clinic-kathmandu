The 3 Components of a Dental Implant

Unlike removable dentures that sit loosely on gums, a dental implant permanently reconstructs both the root and the visible tooth crown.

1. Titanium Fixture (Root)

A medical-grade titanium screw surgically placed into your jawbone. Over 8–12 weeks, your bone cells fuse directly to it through osseointegration.

2. Custom Abutment

A precision-milled titanium or zirconia connector that attaches to the fixture and emerges through the gumline to securely hold the final crown.

3. Monolithic Zirconia Crown

A diamond-hard, metal-free CAD/CAM ceramic tooth custom-shaded to perfectly match your natural teeth and withstand maximum bite forces.

Why Implants Outperform Bridges & Dentures

When a tooth is lost, the underlying jawbone loses stimulation and rapidly shrinks away, causing facial collapse. Traditional dental bridges require grinding down healthy neighbor teeth, and removable dentures slip and slide during meals.

Dental implants are the only restoration that prevents bone loss, stimulating your jawbone naturally while providing 100% original biting strength to enjoy all your favorite Nepali foods freely.

Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step Guided Implant Journey

A sterile, predictable surgical and prosthetic workflow from 3D planning to final crown delivery.

01

3D Digital Bone Assessment

We evaluate your jawbone width, height, and nerve anatomy using high-resolution digital imaging and virtual surgical planning.

02

Painless Surgical Placement

Under gentle local anesthesia, our Oral Surgeon places the titanium fixture into the prepared bone in a comfortable 45-minute visit.

03

Osseointegration (Healing)

Over 8 to 12 weeks, the titanium implant biologically fuses to your living jawbone. Comfortable temporary teeth are provided.

04

Digital 3D Optical Scan

We capture a precise digital impression of the healed implant position to design your custom abutment and monolithic crown.

05

Zirconia Crown Delivery

We attach the custom abutment and permanently secure your final Zirconia crown, restoring 100% natural chewing strength.

Comprehensive guide to titanium dental implants and permanent tooth replacement in Nepal.

A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium screw placed into the jawbone to replace a missing tooth root. Over 3–4 months, it biologically bonds to bone (osseointegration) and supports a permanent CAD/CAM Zirconia crown.

Step 1: 3D CBCT digital scan & planning. Step 2: Implant post placement (30–45 mins). Step 3: Osseointegration healing period (3–4 months). Step 4: Digital intraoral scan and permanent Zirconia crown delivery.
