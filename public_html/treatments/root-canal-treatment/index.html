Signs & Symptoms

When Do You Need a Root Canal?

When bacteria penetrate the dental enamel and dentin into the pulp chamber, nerve inflammation triggers severe symptoms. If you experience any of these, visit us immediately:

Severe Throbbing Pain

Spontaneous, sharp or continuous throbbing tooth pain, especially when lying down or chewing.

Lingering Sensitivity

Prolonged ache and sensitivity that persists for minutes after consuming hot or ice-cold drinks.

Gum Swelling & Boil

Tender, swollen gums or a pimple-like bump (abscess fistula) releasing unpleasant discharge near the tooth root.

Tooth Discoloration

A single front or back tooth turning dark grey or brown following physical trauma or deep cavity decay.

Precision Workflow

Our 4-Step Painless RCT Procedure

Here is how our Endodontic specialists eliminate infection and permanently restore your tooth.

01

Digital RVG & Gentle Anesthesia

We take high-definition digital X-rays to assess root anatomy and administer computerized local anesthesia so you feel zero discomfort.

02

Rotary Cleaning & Apex Measurement

Using flexible motorized nickel-titanium (NiTi) files and digital apex locators, we carefully clean, shape, and debride the entire canal system.

03

Ultrasonic Disinfection & 3D Seal

The root canals are irrigated with antibacterial solutions and hermetically sealed with bio-compatible thermoplastic gutta-percha.

04

Core Build-Up & Protective Crown

The crown structure is rebuilt with nano-hybrid composite and reinforced with a custom Zirconia or PFM crown for lifelong chewing strength.

Clinical Philosophy

Why Save Your Natural Tooth Over Extraction?

While pulling a painful tooth might seem like a quick fix, extracting a tooth causes surrounding teeth to drift, compromises your bite alignment, leads to jawbone loss, and ultimately costs 3x to 5x more for artificial tooth replacement.

  • Preserves natural chewing sensation and bite force
  • Prevents adjacent teeth from shifting out of position
  • Protects natural jawbone structure against atrophy
  • Lasts for decades when capped with a Zirconia crown

Root Canal Treatment (RCT) FAQs

Transparent facts and clinical details on painless root canal therapy in Kathmandu.

The endodontist removes infected pulp tissue from inside the root canals using rotary nickel-titanium instruments, disinfects the canals with ultrasonic irrigants, shapes them, and hermetically seals them with gutta-percha.

A root-canal-treated tooth loses its internal blood supply and becomes brittle over time. A Zirconia or PFM crown encases the tooth, preventing vertical fractures under heavy chewing forces.

Front anterior teeth RCT ranges from NPR 4,500–6,500. Premolars cost NPR 5,500–8,000, and complex multi-rooted molars range from NPR 7,000–12,000 depending on canal curvature and calcification.

Most teeth are completed in 1 to 2 visits. Uninfected or acutely inflamed teeth qualify for Single-Sitting RCT in one 60–90 minute appointment.
