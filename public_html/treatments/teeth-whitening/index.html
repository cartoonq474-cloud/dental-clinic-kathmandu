What Is Teeth Whitening?

Teeth whitening is a cosmetic procedure that lightens stains and discoloration from your teeth enamel. At Dental in Kathmandu, we use medical-grade hydrogen peroxide bleaching gels activated by a specialized blue light. This clinical method safely breaks down deep discolored molecules, brightening your smile by up to 8 shades in a single visit.

Who Needs Teeth Whitening?

This treatment is ideal for individuals looking to enhance their smile aesthetics. Common causes of tooth staining include:

  • Stains from consuming tea, coffee, red wine, or sodas.
  • Discoloration caused by tobacco use or smoking.
  • Natural yellowing of teeth due to aging.
  • Stains from fluoride exposure during tooth development.

Benefits of Professional Whitening

  • ✓ Instant results: Brightens your smile in a single 60-minute session.
  • ✓ Safe procedures: Supervised by dental professionals to protect your gums.
  • ✓ Customized shade: We match the whitening level to your visual preference.
  • ✓ Long-lasting: Results can last up to 1-2 years with proper home care.

Step-by-Step Whitening Process

  • 1

    Preparation & Cleaning

    We check your teeth and gums, then perform a quick clean to remove surface plaque and ensure even bleaching gel application.

  • 2

    Gum Protection

    A protective barrier gel is applied to your gums to safeguard them from the medical-grade bleaching agent.

  • 3

    Gel & Light Activation

    The whitening gel is applied to your teeth and activated using a specialized LED light. This is done in 2-3 cycles of 15 minutes each.

  • 4

    Rinse & Fluoride Care

    The gel and barrier are removed, and we apply a desensitizing fluoride treatment to strengthen your enamel.

Recovery & Aftercare

To maintain your bright smile, we recommend following the "White Diet" for the first 48 hours. Avoid dark foods and drinks (coffee, tea, soy sauce, curries) and smoking. Temporary tooth sensitivity is normal and resolves within 24 hours.
