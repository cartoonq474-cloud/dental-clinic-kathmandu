Stain Science

What Causes Yellow & Discolored Teeth?

Tooth stains accumulate over years and penetrate deep beneath the outer enamel crystals.

Milk Tea & Coffee

Heavy tannins in daily Nepali milk tea (chiya), black coffee, and red wine bond to enamel pellicles.

Tobacco & Nicotine

Tar and nicotine create stubborn brown-yellow stains that settle into microscopic enamel fissures.

⏳

Natural Ageing

As we age, outer white enamel naturally wears thinner, allowing yellow underlying dentin to show through.

Turmeric & Spices

Frequent consumption of deeply pigmented curries, food colorings, and betel nut gradually stains teeth.

Clinical Protocol

Our 5-Step In-Office Whitening Procedure

A relaxing, pain-free 60-minute transformation performed by certified Cosmetic Dentists.

01

Shade Mapping & Polish

We record your initial baseline tooth shade on the VITA guide and polish away surface plaque for maximum gel absorption.

02

Liquid Dam Gum Barrier

A specialized medical resin is painted along your gum margins and light-cured, completely shielding soft tissues from the gel.

03

Medical Whitening Gel

We apply high-potency, pH-balanced bleaching gel evenly across all visible upper and lower smile-zone teeth.

04

3x15 Min Laser Photo-Activation

Cold blue LED and diode laser light activates oxygen molecules in the gel, breaking down deep yellow chromogen stains.

05

Fluoride Seal & Reveal

We rinse the gel, apply a desensitizing fluoride varnish to strengthen enamel, and reveal your dazzling new smile.
