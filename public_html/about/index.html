Zero-Compromise Hygiene

Our 6-Step Hospital Sterilization Protocol

Patient safety is our highest priority. We follow strict international CDC and ISO 9001:2015 compliant sterilization cycles for every single patient visit.

1

Ultrasonic Enzymatic Bath

Instruments are soaked in multi-enzymatic bio-cleaning solutions and treated with ultrasonic vibration to dissolve all microscopic biological residues.

2

Medical Disinfection & Scrubbing

Manual and automated medical-grade scrubbing with neutral enzymatic agents removes surface impurities before high-pressure rinsing.

3

Sealed Pouches with Indicators

Instruments are sealed in hermetic medical-grade sterilization pouches with integrated chemical indicator strips that change color upon full sterility.

4

Vacuum Class-B Autoclaving (134°C)

Steam sterilization in a pressurized vacuum Class-B autoclave chamber at 134°C (2.1 bar) completely eliminates all bacteria, viruses, and heat-resistant spores.

5

Single-Use Disposable Barriers

All patient bibs, suction tips, examination gloves, syringe needles, and chair barrier films are 100% single-use and disposed of after every appointment.

6

Medical Air & Surface Sanitization

Dental operatories and chair surfaces undergo hospital-grade chemical wipe downs, and treatment rooms feature HEPA air filtration between patient sessions.

Modern Technology

State-of-the-Art Dental Technology

Investing in modern digital dentistry allows us to provide more accurate diagnostics, shorter chair times, and completely painless procedures.

HD Intraoral 3D Cameras

High-resolution chairside live imaging lets you clearly see what the dentist sees, enabling collaborative and transparent treatment planning.

Low-Dose Digital Radiography

Instant digital X-ray sensors reduce patient radiation exposure by up to 90% while providing instant high-contrast bone and root visualization.

Rotary Endodontics & Apex Locators

Computerized apex locators and motorized NiTi rotary files enable 100% precise canal shaping, making single-sitting root canals virtually painless.

CAD/CAM 3D Ceramic Milling

Digital computer-aided design and milling for Zirconia crowns and porcelain veneers delivering micron-perfect margins and unbreakable durability.

Our Commitments

The BrightSmile Patient Care Charter

Six foundational standards guaranteed to every patient who walks through our Putalisadak clinic doors.

1. 100% Transparent NPR Pricing

You receive an itemized, written treatment quote before any procedure begins. Zero surprise fees, hidden costs, or unnecessary upsells—ever.

2. Pain-Free Dentistry Protocols

We apply topical numbing gel before any local injection and check in with you continuously during treatments to ensure zero discomfort.

3. Certified Genuine Biomaterials

We exclusively use certified genuine dental biomaterials: German Ivoclar Vivadent IPS E.max®, Swiss Straumann® implants, and GC America composites.

4. Written Quality Warranties

All CAD/CAM Zirconia Crowns and Dental Implants come with official 10 to 15-year warranty documentation for complete patient peace of mind.

5. 24/7 Priority Emergency Triage

Acute dental trauma, fractured crowns, or severe midnight toothaches receive immediate priority phone guidance and rapid clinic chair access.

6. Dedicated Specialist Collaboration

Complex multidisciplinary cases (e.g. implants, full smile makeovers, orthodontics) are jointly reviewed by senior MDS specialists for best clinical outcomes.
